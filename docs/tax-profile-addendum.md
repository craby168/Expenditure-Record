# Tax-profile correction: architectural professional practice

**Prepared 31 October 2026 (Asia/Taipei).** This addendum supersedes the earlier proposal's assumption that the studio is an ordinary sole-proprietor profit-seeking enterprise. The user has clarified that it is an architect's practice, with the chief architect as its sole owner and funder. The supplied logo reads **如果建築師事務所**; exact registered spelling and tax registration have not been independently verified. This research does not identify or authenticate the practice from its logo.

The MVP is now **expense capture and accountant handoff only**. Keep `tax_profile = unconfirmed` until the accountant checks registration and activities. A professional-practice profile is a strong candidate, not an automatic result of the business name or ownership percentage.

## 2. Expense capture tailored to the practice

**Restaurant receipts.** Ask whether the spending was client entertainment (交際費), staff meals (伙食費), business-trip food, or personal consumption. Retain purpose, participants/relationship, project and receipt. Professional-rule §25 permits qualifying entertainment subject to evidence and an annual limit; the current architect limit is **7% of assessed professional revenue**. This is not permission to deduct every restaurant bill, and an expenses-only register lacks the revenue denominator. [§25](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?flno=25&pcode=G0340030).

The sole practitioner's ordinary daily meals are personal living costs, not deductible office meals merely because a receipt exists. Employee meals and genuine business-trip expenses follow different requirements. [MOF explanation of practitioner salary and meals](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/384fb3077bb349ea973e7fc6f13b6974?cntId=5ff42be9d4eb425cb3b8614918549c3e). Suggested UI outcomes are “business context needed,” “personal,” or “accountant review,” not an automatic tax deduction.

**Fuel receipts.** Capture vehicle identifier, business purpose/project or site visit, business/personal use, payment date and the practice's withholding-unit 統一編號 shown on the invoice. Professional-rule §29 ties fuel recognition to business use and prescribed evidence, including the practice ID on fuel-station invoices. [Official professional rules, §29](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0340030). Section 15 specifically addresses mixed family/professional cars and the asset register; it includes a one-half recognition rule for specified mixed-use costs and conditions for more than one car. [§15](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?flno=15&pcode=G0340030). Collect facts and flag review; do not automatically apply 50% to every fuel purchase or claim input VAT.

**General purchases.** Record what was bought and why: stationery, printing, project materials, equipment, repairs, deposits or personal items. Keep quantity, unit price where available, supplier, document ID, total, tax shown, payer, payment status and original image. Equipment or lasting improvements require an asset/capitalization review. Do not carry the prior generic 3% ordinary-receipt ceiling or NT$80,000 asset shortcut into the confirmed professional profile without checking the applicable professional rule and accountant policy. The capture category describes the expense; it does not establish tax acceptance.

**Rare foreign software subscriptions.** Retain supplier country/name, account purchaser, contract/license or plan description, service period, original currency, total and separately shown taxes, payment proof, exchange-rate evidence and business purpose. Flag both foreign-service VAT and income-tax withholding review. VAT Act §36 can impose purchaser obligations for foreign services; its general-VAT business-use exception should not be assumed for a professional practice. [營業稅法 §36](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?flno=36&pcode=G0340080). Income-tax treatment also depends on the actual service, rights and source of income: MOF guidance distinguishes some offshore standardized-software transactions from taxable Taiwan-source electronic services. [MOF cross-border-service explanation](https://www.mof.gov.tw/singlehtml/979b54e408fb499eae3c1d9efe978868?cntId=2568bc038a0848aca447a9138fabbfa1). Do not classify every subscription as a royalty, assume every foreign invoice requires the same withholding, or treat foreign tax shown as Taiwan input credit.

## 3. Documents, retention and accountant output

The relevant professional bookkeeping regulation is **執行業務者帳簿憑證設置取得保管辦法**, not solely the earlier profit-seeking-enterprise regulation. Its rules include daily chronological posting without delay beyond two months, required expense evidence and preservation of native electronic originals. [MOF regulation, §§5 and 8–9](https://law-out.mof.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=FL042260).

Books generally require at least ten years and vouchers at least five years after annual settlement, with unresolved/permanent matters treated separately. Paper-original destruction after electronic conversion has assessment/approval conditions; a photograph alone does not authorize disposal. [Professional bookkeeping regulation, §§11–12](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?PCode=G0340118). Retain original files, paper location, immutable revisions and an export manifest. Keep auto-deletion disabled in the prototype.

Export an **expense register**, evidence files/index, business-purpose notes, payer/payment information, category suggestions and unresolved tax questions. Label totals “recorded expenses” or “reviewed expense records,” never “tax-deductible total,” “net profit” or “final tax due.” The accountant still needs revenue and other records outside this expenses-only MVP to determine income or prepare complete statements. Professional-rule §5 calls for a 財產目錄 and 收支報告表 with individual-income filing; the expense register is supporting input, not those completed statements. [Official professional rules, §5](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=G0340030).

- Registered practice name/spelling, registered address and 統一編號, including its role as a withholding-unit identifier.
- Taxpayer profile: professional practice, ordinary business, or distinct activities requiring separate treatment.
- Individual versus joint professional practice; funding/ownership alone is not a substitute for registration evidence.
- Accounting/tax basis and any approved alternative, applicable filing profile and periods.
- VAT status of each activity, any input-credit entitlement, and foreign-service obligations.
- Employment, payee residency and withholding facts; vehicle ownership/use and listing in the asset register.
- Final tax category, accepted business allocation, deduction amount, year-level limits and tax-year attribution.

Individual consolidated article pages expose differing compilation cutoffs, so effective dates and subsequent announcements must be rechecked before production tax automation.
